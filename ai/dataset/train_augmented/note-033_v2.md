## POS Machine

A computerized replacement for a cash register.

- if tama date, press ENTER
- susi ng officer

POS - STEP 1: Enter Ca number

Type Ca number

Note: Each cashier has their own assigned number

- Enter

STEP 2: If with suki card; Press SUKI key

3: Insert suki card in the suki card reader (burahan yung gold chip ng eraser pag di umilaw yung reader)

4: Press Order Recall

5: Enter Order Number

6: Press TOTAL, enter MODE of PAYMENT (EM-Pay for electronic)
   enter MONEY TENDERED (add 00 for centavo)

7: Receipt will be printed. Give exact change.

8: Wait for the prompt, "You may now remove the suki card"

9: Remove suki card and put on tray

## FORWARD AND REFILL * expiry - 4 months

CLEANING OF GONDOLAS

- Employee must maintain cleanliness and orderliness of assigned gondolas / shelves at all times.
- Cleaning of gondolas and stocks is to be done every week or as needed, general cleaning should be done monthly & should be monitored or recorded in the log book.
- Cleaning of gondola should always start from top to bottom.

PROCEDURE

1. Prepare the following cleaning materials:
  
   - Detergent powder
   - Cleaning absorbent cloth (microfiber / franela)
   - Scrubbing pad
   - Medium-sized pail, half-filled w/ water
   - Clean dry absorbent cloth (microfiber / franela)

2. Transfer all the stocks on the lower most layer of the gondola to clean tote boxes / push cart / boxes.
