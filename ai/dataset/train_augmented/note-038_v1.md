2. Liniment - solution / mixture of various substances in oil, alcoholic solutions of soap / emulsions and may contain suitable antimicrobial preservatives.
3. Emulsion - a fine dispersion of minute droplets of one liquid in another in w/c it is not soluble or miscible.
4. Suspension - a two-phase system consisting of a finely divided solid dispersed in a solid, liquid or gas. (powder, tanong if titimpla na)
5. Lotion - either liquid or semi-liquid preparation w/c contains one / more active ingredients in an appropriate vehicle.

## C. SEMI-SOLID

1. Ointment - oil base preparation generally applied to dry / scaly skin.
2. Cream - water-base: preparation generally applied to weeping / wet surfaces.
3. Paste - an external medicament that has a stiffer consistency than an ointment and is less greasy because of its higher percentage of powdered ingredients. (not generally suited for hairy parts of the body)
