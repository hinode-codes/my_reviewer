## COMMON TERMINOLOGIES ENCOUNTERED IN THE BRANCH

COBRA - Call Other Branch

DALPO - Do All Possible

TOSPRO - Temporarily Out of Stock Please Re-order

HATSYA - Happy To Serve You Attitude
  Kahit Wala Meron

FEFO - First Expiry First Out

FIFO - First In First Out (* naunang dineliver)

UAC - Understanding Acceptance and Correctness (* ✓ product, O item no., show expiration date, - count product)

Take ord - Computer in retail counter use to take order (gamot lang)

POS - Point of Sales - Cash Register / Kaha

VFD - Vacuum Flourescent Display

Fogel / Bio Ref - Biological Refrigerator (* 2-8° lang dapat)

SEED - Stock Easy on the Eye Display (* SS (pretty display))

SKU - Stock Keeping Unit (* ilan laman)

Gondola - shelves where stocks are displayed
  - wall gondola / shelf gondola

Basket - container used to hold / carry purchases of customer in the SS section

Dispensing tray - container used to hold / carry purchases of customer in the RS section

Tote box - box for storing, handling / transporting products from the Distribution Center (red big box)

Diser - Merchandisers who monitor products display in the store

RS - Retail Section

SS - Self Service Section

Labas - Self Service Section

Loob - Retail Section

Bandera - Colored Paper / Flag (* paper na nakadikit sa gondola (pink / green), white - bagong item)

Bangka - container of loose tablets on the gondola (red boxes in gondola) (* loose - bawas na meds sa banig)

Baril - handheld scanner in the retail section

Mais - customers who buy small amounts (tingi)

Jackpot - customers who buy large amounts (bulto - maramihan)

Conventional store - branch w/out self-service section (RS only)

Super store - branch w/ complete line of products (can be 24-hr or non 24 hr) (* complete)

OCNR - order confirmed not recalled (unrecalled) + di napunch sa kaha

* di lahat ng gamot pwedeng i-loose (bawasan) * walang loose - ask muna
