## SERVICE HINTS

*

1. Carry the article to the customer if possible. Do not make the customer come to you.
2. Always be kind to children and always speak to a child when he/she is accompanied by their parents.
3. If you happen to know that there is sickness in a customer's family, inquire about the patient's welfare.
4. Do not discuss your personal problems w/ a customer, but always listen sympathetically to what the customer has to say.
5. Show real interest in your customer no matter how hard he/she is to get along with.
6. Go out of your way to please the customer no matter how hard he/she is to get along w/.
7. Avoid hurrying a customer or showing a hurried attitude. Be smart and quick in supplying his/her wants but don't ever say "who's next?" while handling him/her, his/her change.
8. Always keep yourself looking neat and clean.
9. Never give harsh or careless answers to customers. Never be faward or fresh.
10. Always speak well of the company.
11. Do not let customers leave dissatisfied if you can prevent it. Dissatisfied customers shut you and your company.
12. Give all the customers the same good service regardless of w/er they are well-dressed or poorly dressed, cultured or rough, white, brown, black, or w/er the amount of purchase is one peso or one hundred pesos.
13. Try to develop a pleasing personality. You can measure your success in this by the number of ppl who like to be served by you.
14. Be kind, patient, and considerate to the aged, illiterate and physically defective. They will appreciate you for it and others will admire you for it.
