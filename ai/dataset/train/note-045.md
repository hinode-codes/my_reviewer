## TYPE A OFFENSES (4 times)

1. Tardiness and/or undertime committed six (6) times or more w/in a thirty day period.
2. Tardiness and/or undertime that accumulates to more than two (2) hours or one hundred twenty (120) minutes w/in any thirty day (30) period.
3. Three (3) unexcused absences (whether whole-day / half-day absence w/in 30-day period).

## TYPE B OFFENSES (3)

1. Leaving premise w/out permission
2. Disregard office directives
3. Loafing / loitering during office hours, sleeping on duty, malingering to avoid work
4. Entering restricted area
5. Insulting / abusive language
6. Gambling
7. Smoking
8. + 15 min. break
9. Damage / lose vehicle
10. Stay in unauthorized hours

## TYPE C OFFENSES (2)

1. Negligent, lose company time / money, performing someone else's work, not doing duties
2. Destroying, damaging, losing company property
3. Unexcused absence on a rest day
4. Soliciting contributions
5. Extending credit w/out approval
6. Using letterhead unauthorized
7. Unauthorized use of phone
8. Non-wearing of uniform / ID after the fifth (5th) reprimand

## Type D Offenses

1. Serving orders w/out receipts
