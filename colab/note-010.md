## Contributing Factors to Antibiotics Resistance

• unnecessary antibiotic prescribing practices
• use of antibiotics to prevent disease ("PROPHYLAXIS")
• physician "softening" to patient demand for antibiotics or due to pharmaceutical detailing
• non-compliance to clinical treatment guidelines
• lack of knowledge on the factors that increase resistance

## Common MISCONCEPTIONS

1. Antibiotics should be given immediately for:
   • Sore throat • High fever • yellow / green nasal discharge • productive cough
   • Diarrhea
   * These symptoms are not automatically bacterial in nature so antibiotics are not the primary option.
2. If you already took a "weak" antibiotic, you need to take a "stronger" one next time.
   • If prescribed and taken correctly, there is no weak or stronger antibiotic.
3. Isoniazid (INH) is a good vitamin for the lungs.
   • Isoniazid is an antibiotic for tuberculosis and not a vitamin.
