## B. LIQUIDS

1. Solution
   a. Aqueous solution - uses water as solvent
      a.1. Douche - a device used to introduce a stream of water into the body. (has applicator, inside vagina)
      a.2. Enema - a rectal injection employed to evacuate the bowels.
      a.3. Mouthwash - a liquid used for rinsing the mouth or gargling, typically containing an antiseptic.
           • therapeutic - formulated to reduce plaque, gingivitis, dental caries and stomatitis.
           • cosmetic - formulated to reduce bad breath through the use of antimicrobial and/or flavoring agents.
      a.4. Gargle - frequently contains antiseptics, antibiotics, and/or anesthetics used for treating the pharynx and the nasopharynx.
      a.5. Juice - prepared from fresh ripe fruit, aqueous in character and is used in making syrups, w/c are employed as vehicles.
   b. Viscid or Thick Aqueous solution - having a thick, sticky consistency
      b.1. Syrup - concentrated solution of sugar such as sucrose in water or other aqueous liquid.
      b.2. Honey - thick liquid preparation somewhat allied to the syrups, differing in that, honey, instead of sugar, is used as a base.
      b.3. Jelly - a class of gels in which the structural coherent matrix contains a high portion of liquid, usually water.
   c. Non-Aqueous solution - water is not the solvent
      c.1. Tincture - type of medicine extracted from a plant in an alcohol solution.
      c.2. Inhalation - drug / solution by the nasal or respiratory route for either a local / systemic effect
      c.3. Spirit - alcoholic / hydroalcoholic solution of volatile substances, sometimes known as essences.
