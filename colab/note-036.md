## A. SOLIDS

1. Tablet - prepared either by compression or molding methods. May be oral, buccal, sublingual, intravaginal.
   a. Scored Tablet - a tablet with a score. Can be divided, chewed or crushed
   b. Sugar-coated Tablet - there is a thick, hard coating of sugar surrounding the tablet.
   c. Film-coated Tablet - very thin layers of a safe ingredient placed around the tablet.
   Note: Sugar and Film coatings are used to:
   • hide the flavor of particularly unpleasant tasting drugs.
   • prevents light and moisture from entering the tablet, w/c causes the drug to break down quickly.
   d. Enteric-coated tablet - has special coating wherein active ingredient is not dissolved in the stomach but instead in the small intestine preventing the irritation of the stomach.
   e. Chewable tablet - has a smooth, rapid disintegration when chewed or allowed to dissolve in the mouth.
2. Capsule - the drug substance is enclosed in either a hard or soft soluble container or shell of a suitable form of gelatin.
3. Spansule - extended release capsule containing spherical multi-colored granules.
