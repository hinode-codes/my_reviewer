* Immunity - is natural / acquired resistance to disease
* Immunization - means of providing immunity by using a biologic
  * vaccination is the term commonly used
* Antibody - a protein produced by the body's immune system when it detects harmful substances called antigens
* Antigen - substance that stimulates the production of antibodies
* Immunoglobulin - special concentrated antibody preparations w/ provide immediate short-term protection for individuals who are at high risk of experiencing severe disease or of developing serious complications from the disease
* Vaccine - a suspension of attenuated (live) or inactivated (killed) microorganisms / fractions thereof administered to induce immunity / prevent infectious diseases
* Booster Dose - supplementary dose to increase the effectiveness of an immunizing agent
* Toxin - a poison produced by a living organism especially by a bacterium
* Antitoxin - an antibody produced by the body to counteract a toxin formed by invading bacteria / from any other source
* Toxoid - a modified bacterial toxin (exotoxin) that has been rendered non-toxic (detoxified) but retains ability to stimulate the formation of antitoxin and prevent bacterial toxicities
  * toxoids are used as vaccines

## NATURAL IMMUNITY

1. Species Immunity - in general, cold-blooded animals are not susceptible to diseases common to warm-blooded animals
   * humans are not all susceptible to certain diseases of lower animals
2. Racial Immunity - certain races are more resistant / susceptible to specific diseases
3. Individual Immunity - individuals vary in the ability to resist
