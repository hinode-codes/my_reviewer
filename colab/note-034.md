## CLASSIFICATION OF DRUGS ACCORDING TO USE

* in EXAM (Identity, T/F, multiple)

1. Analgesic - relieves pain
2. Anesthetic - temporarily destroys sensation of pain
3. Antacid - neutralizes acidity of stomach
4. Anthelmintic - expels intestinal worms
5. Anti-amebic - expels ameba
6. Antianemic - prevents anemia
7. Antiarrhythmic - corrects irregularities in the heartbeat
8. Anti-asthmatic - relieves asthma
9. Antibiotic / Anti-infective - substance produced by a living microorganism w/c has the capacity to inhibit the growth or even kill other microorganisms.
10. Anticoagulant - prevents the clotting of blood
11. Anticonvulsant / antiepileptic - treatment of epileptic seizures
12. Antidiabetic - lowers / controls blood sugar
13. Antidiarrheal - stops diarrhea or loose bowel movement (LBM)
14. Antiemetic - prevents vomiting
15. Antiflatulent - prevents generation of gas in the intestine / stomach
16. Antifungal - treats fungal infections
17. Antiglaucoma - alleviates / prevents glaucoma
18. Anti-gout - lowers uric acid level in the blood
19. Anti-hemorrhoidal - shrinks hemorrhoids
20. Antihistamine - stops allergy
21. Antihyperlipidemic - reduces cholesterol level
22. Antihypertensive - lowers high blood pressure
23. Anti-inflammatory - reduces and prevents swelling of tissues
24. Antimalarial - prevents or treats malaria
25. Antineoplastic - treats various types of cancer
26. Anti-obesity - reduces excess fat in the body
27. Antipruritic - relieves itching
28. Antipsoriatic - treat psoriasis
29. Antipyretic - lowers body temperature
30. Antirheumatic - relieves rheumatic pain
31. Antiseptic - chemical that inhibits the growth of bacteria
32. Antispasmodic - relieves spasm of smooth muscle in the gut
33. Antitussive - stops cough
34. Anti-ulcer - inhibits acid secretion of the stomach
35. Antivertigo - prevents / relieves dizziness
