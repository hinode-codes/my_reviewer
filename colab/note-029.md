## DRUG LABEL

• Product Name
  > Generic Name (should be enclosed in a box)
  > Brand Name (if applicable)
• Generic name of the products to appear prominently & above the brand name in all product labels in compliance to Republic Act No. 6675 (Generic Act of 1988)
• Dosage Form and Strength
• Pharmacologic Category
• Formulation / Composition
• Indication(s)
• Dosage & Mode of Administration
• Contraindication(s), Precaution(s), Warning(s), if applicable
• Interactions • Adverse Drug Reaction(s) • Overdose & Treatment
• Storage Condition(s) • Net Content / Pack size
• Name & Address of the Marketing Authorization Holder (MAH)
• Name & Address of the Manufacturer
• For prescription drug products: Rx symbol & caution statement
• Registration Number • Batch number & lot number (if any)
• Expiration Date & Date of Manufacture

## STANDARD SELLING & CASHIERING PROCEDURE (SSP)

G - Greet the customer to make him / her feel welcome.
R - Recognize customer concern and Request for Suki Card. (offer suki card)
E - Extract information, take customer & give price for customer's order. (UAC)
A - Attend to customer's order w/ care and accuracy and give exact change.
T - Thank the customer for his / her continuous patronage.

## G.R.E.A.T. in SELF-SERVICE SECTION

G - Greet the customer to make him feel welcome.
R - Request for "Suki Card".
E - Endorse company-owned products, scan items & give price for customer's purchase.
A - Attend to customer's order with care and accuracy, and give exact change.
T - Thank the customer for his continuous patronage.
