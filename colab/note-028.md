## TAKE ORDER MACHINE

(magpasok ng order)
A machine located at the Retail Section where the order of the customer is entered and where the price and stock information of the pharmaceutical product can be viewed.

## KEYS AND FUNCTIONS

Alt Tab - to start takeord station
Any key - regular transaction
F2 - Senior Citizen Transaction (SC)
F4 - Person w/ Disability Transaction (PWD)
F6 - Brand / Generic Name
F8 - Suki Senior
