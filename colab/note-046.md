### B. MEASURE BY WEIGHT

| | SUPPLEMENT | |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 1000 mcg = 1 mg | 250 mg = 1/4 or 0.25 g | 1 1/2 grs = 90 - 100 mg |
| 1000 mg = 1 g | 500 mg = 1/2 or 0.50 g | 1 gr = 60 - 65 mg |
| 1000 g = 1 kilo | 10 grs = 600 - 650 mg | 1/2 gr = 30 mg |
| | 5 grs = 300 - 325 mg | 1/4 gr = 15 mg |

APPROXIMATE
* 1 oz = 30 g
* 16 oz = 1 lb
* 2.2 lbs = 1 kg
