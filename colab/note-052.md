The Comprehensive Dangerous Drugs Act of 2002 covers the following products

1. List of Drugs included in Schedule I of the 1961 UN Convention on Narcotic Drugs as amended by the 1972 Protocol (Narcotic / Dangerous Drugs)
2. List of Drugs in Schedule IV of the 1971 UN Convention on Psychotropic Substances (Injectable Psychotropics / Dangerous Drugs)
3. List of Drugs in Schedule IV of the 1971 UN Convention on Psychotropic Substances (Oral Psychotropics / Dangerous Drugs)
4. Substances Controlled Under 1988 UN Convention Against Illicit Traffic on Narcotic Drugs and psychotropic substances
   a) Precursors b) Essential chemicals

NARCOTIC

- substance that produces stupor changes one's sense of pain and dulls the senses in moderate doses. In poisonous doses, it can cause convulsions, coma, or death
