## GLANDS OF THE ENDOCRINE SYSTEM AND HORMONES THEY PRODUCE

### ① PITUITARY GLAND

- called the 'master gland' as the hormones it produces control so many different processes in the body
- it senses the body's needs and sends signals to different organs / glands throughout the body to regulate their function / maintain an appropriate environment

### ③ THYMUS

- the role of these hormones is to make sure a person develops a healthy immune system

### ④ PINEAL GLAND

- it produces and regulates some hormones, including melatonin. Melatonin is best known for the role it plays in regulating sleep patterns.

### ⑤ TESTES

- responsible for making testosterone, the primary male sex hormone, and for producing sperm

### ⑥ OVARIES

- the primary female reproductive organs
- it produces egg cells

### ⑦ THYROID

- produces a hormone called triiodothyronine, known as T3
- it also produces a hormone called thyroxine, known as T4
- Together these hormones regulate your body's temperature, metabolism / heart rate

### ⑧ PANCREAS

- regulates blood sugar

### ⑨ ADRENAL GLANDS

- produce hormones that help regulate your metabolism, immune system, bloodpressure, response to stress and other essential functions

### ⑩ PARATHYROID

- continuously monitor and regulate blood calcium levels.
