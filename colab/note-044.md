1. Toxic / Poisonous Dose - is the amount of drug w/c will cause harmful effect that is not fatal but will cause untoward effect.
2. Lethal / Fatal Dose - is the amount of drug w/c will cause death, and under certain conditions, the amount given may be less than the max. dose, but will produce undesirable effect.

## Factors Affecting the Dosage of Drugs

1. Age - Children are more susceptible to drugs w/c act on the nervous system. They are more sensitive to all forms of drugs than adults.
2. Sex - Women should theoretically receive a smaller amount of dose from 1/2 to 4/5. Men as a rule require larger doses than women and this may be due to the difference in weight.
3. Weight - A large, strong, and healthy child should get more than a weak and sickly child of the same age. If the individual is overweight, the dose should be higher due to the presence of fatty tissues, and if the individual is underweight, the dose may be lower because of less active tissues.
4. Temperament - Nervous persons require smaller dose of drugs used as stimulants.
5. Nature of the disease - the more complicated / severe the disease is, the larger the dose.
6. Manner of administration - If the drug is injected, the dose is smaller.
7. Habit - it is the established custom of taking drug frequently, so that the gradual increase of the dose is required to produce normal effect of the drug.
