## LARGE VOLUME PARENTERALS

These solutions are usually administered by intravenous infusion to replenish body fluids / electrolytes or to provide nutrition.
Employed in maintenance therapy for the patient entering / recovering from surgery and for the patient who is unconscious and unable to take fluids, electrolytes, and nutrition orally.
